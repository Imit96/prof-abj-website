# Updating your website

You can change everything on the site yourself, from a browser, without touching any code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-site-address/keystatic** (bookmark it).
2. Sign in with the account you were invited with.
3. The menu on the left lists everything you can edit.

When you press **Save**, the change goes live within about a minute.

## What's where

| To change… | Open |
|---|---|
| Your biography, photo, email addresses, CV file, citation numbers | **Profile & contact** |
| Appointments, degrees, honours, societies, editorial roles, talks | **Career, honours & service** |
| The six research themes | **Research themes** |
| Grants and projects | **Grants & projects** |
| Publications | **Publications** |
| Places on the world map | **Institutions & places** |
| Collaborators | **Collaborators** |
| Photos | **Gallery** (the Gallery page appears once there's at least one photo) |
| News items on the homepage | **News** |
| The Foundation page | **Foundation page** (tick "Show the Foundation page" when it's ready) |

## Common tasks

**Update your citation numbers.** In **Profile & contact → Research metrics**, change the numbers and the "as of" month.

**Replace your CV.** In **Profile & contact → CV file**, upload the new file (a PDF is best) and update "CV last updated".

**Add a publication.** New papers on your ORCID record appear on the site automatically. To add one that isn't on ORCID, open **Publications → Add**, fill in the title, authors, journal, year and DOI, then save. Tick **Feature on homepage** to show it in "Selected publications".

**Remove a publication.** Open it and tick **Hide from website**. Don't delete it: anything still on your ORCID record would come back.

**Add a photo.** Open **Gallery → Add**, choose the photo, write a short caption and a one-line description for people using screen readers, pick a category, then save.

**Show or hide your phone number.** Use the **Show phone number on the website** box in **Profile & contact**. It is off by default.

**Add a place to the map.** You need its latitude and longitude. Search the institution on Google Maps, right-click the pin, and the first item is the two numbers. Paste the first number into **Latitude** and the second into **Longitude**.
